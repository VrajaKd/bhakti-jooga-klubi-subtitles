Bhakti Jooga Klubi Subtitles

A private tool used by the YouTube channel Bhakti jooga klubi to publish its own lectures in more languages.

It transcribes the channel's Estonian lectures, translates them, and uploads the resulting subtitle tracks and translated titles and descriptions to the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

It is not a public service: it is operated only by the channel owner, for the channel owner's videos.
